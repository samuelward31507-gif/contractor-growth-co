06FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to get my website live? +
Most websites are designed, built, and live within 7–14 business days of your onboarding call. We move fast so you start getting leads sooner.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract? +
No long-term contracts. Your monthly plan continues month-to-month. We earn your business every month by delivering results.
What if I already have a website? +
We'll do a free audit of your current site. If it's worth keeping and improving, we can work with it. If a fresh build is the better move, we'll tell you exactly why.
Will I need to learn complicated software? +
No. Everything is done for you. You get a simple mobile dashboard to see your leads and stats — no technical experience needed whatsoever.
What does "done for you" actually mean? +
We build and set up everything — website, automations, chatbot, review requests, follow-up sequences. You don't touch a thing. Once it's live, we handle maintenance too.
How does the 30-day guarantee work? +
Simple. If you're not happy with what we built in the first 30 days, tell us and we'll make it right. No hassle, no runaround.
Can I upgrade my package later? +
Absolutely. You can upgrade from Starter to Growth or from Growth to Pro at any time. You'll only pay the difference in setup fees.
